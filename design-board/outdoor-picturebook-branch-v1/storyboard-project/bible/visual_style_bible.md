# Visual Style Bible

## style_night_comic_b_v1

- Medium: original hand-drawn wordless night comic illustration
- Production status: user-approved visual contract for `FORMAL-PICTUREBOOK-FULLFRAME-V1-A`. Root R4, formal stargaze F1–F5 and the Home ritual visual set are exact-file frozen anchors. `STARGAZE-FORMAL-BATCH-V1-A` now has `GATE B VISUAL SUBPACKAGE PASS / NOT IN BUILD`: F1 R1, F2 R1, F3 R2, F4 R1 and F5 are all user-approved and frozen. No Cocos/runtime authorization is implied.
- Genre: quiet interactive picture book with one clear event and response per image
- Color logic: a deep-indigo limited palette built from near-black indigo, dark blue, muted blue-gray, and paper-tinted light; one restrained amber family is reserved for the warm door and its interior glimpse
- Value design: large readable light/dark masses before small detail; silhouettes must remain clear at 25% scale
- Ink language: dry-brush comic ink with irregular pressure, broken edges, and purposeful gaps; avoid uniform vector outlines and dark halo contours
- Texture / grain: restrained halftone plus fine paper tooth shared by sky, people, cat, grass, house, and light; no photographic noise or heavy full-screen speckle
- Lighting rules: cool starlight makes only thin rim accents on hair, shoulders, cat ears, roof, and grass; the right-side door is the only obvious warm source; two flowers glow much more weakly
- Night sky: ordinary deep-blue negative space dominates; exactly one broad, faint natural Milky Way with loose edges, dark texture, and visible breaks; sparse stars with only 8–10 visually important points
- Stargaze focus: in `STARGAZE-SKY-FOCUS-V1-A`, panels 1 and 5 hold about 85% sky and panels 2–4 are sky-detail inserts. Render uneven star-dust density, irregular dust-dark rifts, restrained light knots, and blue breathing space; the adult and cat remain only scale and companionship anchors.
- Environment anchor: sloped grass foreground, distant low hills, an original small house in the right middle ground, one stable warm door, and exactly two weakly glowing flowers near the seated pair
- Home ritual anchor: `HOME-MEAL-RITUAL-V1-A` uses the same dry-brush night-comic print language inside a fully readable warm home. H1 and H5 share the same 32–35mm room geography; H2 is the left-wall coat-hook close view; H3 is a connected kitchen medium view that keeps at least one doorway, wall-corner or table-edge anchor back to the main room and preserves the same wall/floor material, woodwork and warm-light direction; H4 is a table close view; H5 is the byte-identical approved wide-room frame. Warmth comes from broad wall/ceiling/floor bounce and ordinary food preparation, never from orange bloom or dark-corner contrast.
- Camera language: portrait `390×844` logic; vary wide, medium, close, and sky-dominant framings so adjacent frames are not near-duplicates; keep the spatial axis and emotional geography readable
- Composition: the adult and cat begin lower-left, the warm door stays on the right whenever visible, and sky negative space remains breathable
- Stargaze hierarchy: sky first, small paired silhouettes second, warm door third in wide anchor panels; sky-detail inserts may crop out ground anchors without relocating them
- Stargaze finale exception: after the F5 shooting-star-free clean frame holds for 0.9s, `STARGAZE-SKY-FINALE-V1-A` permits exactly one restrained meteor overlay from upper-right toward the open sky above the pair, followed by its short fading trail and the approved two-line copy. No other frame or branch may contain a shooting star.
- Panel behavior: artwork is full-bleed per image. Contact sheets may use clean neutral gutters, but gutters, labels, page numbers, and captions are never part of the artwork
- Interaction text: `看看星空`, `吹吹风`, `回家`, the approved finale copy `一颗流星，刚刚从夜里经过。` / `回家，还是再坐一会儿？`, and the home-ritual overlays `放下外衣`, `吃一点`, `喝口温水` are independent browser-board/runtime UI overlays only and must not be rendered into any illustration
- Avoid: watercolor glow ribbons, aurora, neon, multiple Milky Ways, constellations, shooting stars except the single approved F5 finale overlay, meteor showers or loops, cross-shaped sparkle effects, bloom, glossy 3D rendering, flat geometric vector art, generic mascot anatomy, decorative fantasy plants, black room corners, or reward-like light bursts
- Rights boundary: no living-artist names, no “in the exact style of” phrasing, no existing IP characters, no brand logos, and no real-person likeness
- Approved references: verbal B direction plus the user-approved R1 full-frame root master (SHA-256 `5c84ce815ba011cd9be570889c768573e80c3d55d6b59fb2bd211301f86c74e3`) as Batch 1 visual anchor. For home F5, `FORMAL-UI-V1.2-A` supplies only broad warm-light, readable-room, prepared-dinner, and cold-window semantics; its exact pixels, room geometry, furniture, fixtures, or character design must not be copied.
