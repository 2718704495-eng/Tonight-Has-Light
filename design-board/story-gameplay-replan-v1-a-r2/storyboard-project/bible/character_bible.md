# Character Bible — R2 locked invariants

- The adult is an anonymous adult, shown from the back or a controlled three-quarter back view. Keep ordinary adult anatomy, short hair, relaxed shoulders, loose unmarked gray-blue outer layer, dark knit beneath, long trousers, and flat shoes.
- Keep no named identity, no stated gender, no public relationship, no face portrait, no real-person likeness, and no branded clothing.
- The companion is an ordinary domestic cat: a natural quadruped with a compact body, four paws, triangular ears, and one long tail. It is never clothed, upright, speaking, human-faced, or mascot-like.
- Root scale and relationship stay quiet: adult left, cat immediately right, both looking into the same sky. The cat remains around lower-calf height while seated.
- Indoors, the same outer layer moves only from the adult to the left-wall hook; the dark knit, adult scale, cat scale, and unnamed shared attention stay continuous.
