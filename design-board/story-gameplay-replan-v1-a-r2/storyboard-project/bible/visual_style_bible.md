# Visual Style Bible — R2 locked invariants

- B night-comic: an original, full-bleed, wordless picture-book image language with deep indigo, warm ochre, dry-brush ink, and restrained halftone over a shared paper tooth.
- Outdoor value and feeling are outdoor cool/quiet: broad deep-blue negative space, cool thin star rims, a stable right-side warm door, and two much weaker flowers.
- The sky contains one natural Milky Way only: broad, faint, broken, loose-edged, with dark rifts and sparse stars. Do not add aurora ribbons, constellations, extra meteors, Bloom, or glossy 3D effects.
- Indoors are indoor bright/warm/no black corners: broad warm wall, ceiling, and floor bounce keeps the full home, connected kitchen, table, adult, and cat readable. Do not use a dark room or a lights-switch-on performance.
- Text, icons, interaction labels, and the one meteor/copy finale remain separate formal overlays, never baked into an illustration.
