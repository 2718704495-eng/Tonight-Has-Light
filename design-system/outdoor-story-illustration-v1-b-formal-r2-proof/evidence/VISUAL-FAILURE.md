# STORY-ILLUSTRATION-B-FORMAL-R2-PROOF visual failure

Status: `FAIL / REVIEW-BLOCKED / STOPPED AFTER R2.1`

## Controller review

The R2.1 correction improved the amount of sky, removed the strongest wallpaper texture, reduced the house and flowers, and replaced repeated foreground triangles with finer curved grass strokes. Those changes did not reach the approved B quality floor.

## Blocking defects

1. **Adult posture and anatomy** — the lower body is obscured by the slope, so the figure reads as an upright half-body silhouette instead of a seated adult with a visible pelvis, bent leg, and grounded weight. The hand exists but does not resolve the overall posture.
2. **Ordinary-cat anatomy** — shrinking the cat reduced its dominance but made it more icon-like. The neck, chest, arched back, haunch, rear leg, paws, and grounded tail root do not read as one natural skeleton at phone size.
3. **Milky Way structure** — removing the hard segmented tube left a thin dotted arc. The result is not the approved broad, faint, loose-edged Milky Way with natural density changes and dark internal breaks.
4. **Medium mismatch** — named dry-brush, halftone, and paper layers exist, but the visible masses and contours still read as constructed vector art. This remains materially farther from the approved mature night comic than the project quality line permits.
